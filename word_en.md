## 答辩问答

| 问题 | 答案 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
|什么是F1分数？请解释一下F1分数。\nWhat is the F1 score? Please explain it.|For a binary classifier, the output is usually a probability score. We compare this score with a threshold, which is usually 0.5. If the probability score is higher than the threshold, we predict the sample as positive. Otherwise, we predict it as negative.\nThen, we compare the predicted label with the real label. This gives us four possible outcomes: true positive (TP), false positive (FP), true negative (TN), and false negative (FN).\nThe F1 score is calculated from precision and recall.\nPrecision = TP / (TP + FP)\nRecall = TP / (TP + FN)\nThe F1 score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall:\nF1 = 2 × (Precision × Recall) / (Precision + Recall)\nIt can also be written as:\nF1 = 2TP / (2TP + FP + FN)|对于二分类器，输出通常是一个概率分数。我们将这个分数与一个阈值（通常是0.5）进行比较：如果概率分数高于阈值，就把该样本预测为正类；否则预测为负类。\n然后，把预测标签与真实标签进行比较，这样会得到四种可能的结果：真阳性(TP)、假阳性(FP)、真阴性(TN)、假阴性(FN)。\nF1分数由精确率（Precision）和召回率（Recall）计算得出。\n精确率 = TP / (TP + FP)\n召回率 = TP / (TP + FN)\nF1分数是精确率和召回率的调和平均数：\nF1 = 2 × (精确率 × 召回率) / (精确率 + 召回率)\n也可以写成：\nF1 = 2TP / (2TP + FP + FN)|
|本节的主要目的是什么？\nWhat is the main purpose of Section 1?|The main purpose is to prove that the ranking changes between two methods can be found exactly.\nWe show that the equality of two binary macro-F1 scores can be reduced to a polynomial equation with degree at most three.\nTherefore, we can find all possible crossing points without using a grid search.|本节的主要目的是证明两个方法之间的排名变化可以被精确地找出来。\n我们证明，两个二分类macro-F1分数相等的条件，可以转化为一个次数最多为三次的多项式方程。\n因此，我们不需要使用网格搜索，就能找到所有可能的交叉点。|
|什么是「成对相等」（pairwise equality）？\nWhat does "pairwise equality" mean?|Pairwise equality means that we compare two methods at a time.\nFor example, we compare Method A and Method B and find the value of \(\lambda\) where their macro-F1 scores are equal.|成对相等指的是每次比较两个方法。\n例如，我们比较方法A和方法B，找出使它们的macro-F1分数相等的 \(\lambda\) 值。|
|为什么要用多项式？\nWhy do you use a polynomial?|The original macro-F1 formula contains fractions, so it is not easy to find all crossing points directly.\nWe transform the equality into a polynomial equation.\nThen we can use exact root-finding methods to find all crossing points.|原始的macro-F1公式包含分数，直接求出所有交叉点并不容易。\n我们把这个相等关系转化为一个多项式方程。\n这样就可以用精确的求根方法找到所有交叉点。|
|为什么多项式的次数最多是三次？\nWhy is the degree at most three?|Along the affine path, the confusion masses are linear functions of \(\lambda\).\nThe error term is also linear, while the denominator has degree at most two.\nWhen we compare two methods, we multiply a linear term by a quadratic term.\nTherefore, the resulting polynomial has degree at most three.|沿着仿射路径，混淆矩阵的各项质量是 \(\lambda\) 的线性函数。\n误差项也是线性的，而分母的次数最多为二次。\n当我们比较两个方法时，相当于用一个线性项乘以一个二次项。\n因此，最终得到的多项式次数最多为三次。|
|\(\lambda\) 的含义是什么？\nWhat is the meaning of \(\lambda\)?|\(\lambda\) controls how the group weights change.\nWhen \(\lambda=0\), we use the empirical group weights.\nWhen \(\lambda=1\), we use the reference group weights.\nValues between zero and one represent the transition between these two compositions.|\(\lambda\) 控制着组权重如何变化。\n当 \(\lambda=0\) 时，使用的是经验组权重。\n当 \(\lambda=1\) 时，使用的是参考组权重。\n介于0和1之间的取值，代表这两种组成之间的过渡状态。|
|什么是「交叉点」（crossing point）？\nWhat is a "crossing point"?|A crossing point is a value of \(\lambda\) where two methods have exactly the same macro-F1 score.\nAt this point, their ranking can potentially change.|交叉点是指使两个方法的macro-F1分数完全相等的 \(\lambda\) 值。\n在这个点上，它们的排名有可能发生变化。|
|是不是每个交叉点都意味着排名反转？\nDoes every crossing point mean a ranking reversal?|No. A crossing point does not always mean a ranking reversal.\nIf the two curves only touch and then move apart, the ranking does not change.\nWe call this a tangential tie.\nIf the curves cross each other, it is a ranking reversal.|不是。交叉点并不总是意味着排名反转。\n如果两条曲线只是相切然后又分开，排名并不会改变。\n我们把这种情况称为「相切平局」（tangential tie）。\n如果两条曲线真正互相穿过，那才是排名反转。|
|什么是重数（multiplicity），它为什么重要？\nWhat is multiplicity, and why is it important?|Multiplicity tells us how many times a root is repeated.\nAn odd multiplicity usually means that the sign changes around the root, so it indicates a ranking reversal.\nAn even multiplicity means that the sign does not change, so it indicates a tangential tie.|重数表示一个根重复出现的次数。\n奇数重数通常意味着该根附近符号发生了变化，因此代表排名反转。\n偶数重数意味着符号没有变化，因此代表相切平局。|
|什么是有理隔离区间（rational isolating interval）？\nWhat is a rational isolating interval?|A rational isolating interval is a small interval that contains exactly one real root.\nInstead of giving only an approximate value, we give an interval that guarantees where the root is located.|有理隔离区间是一个恰好只包含一个实根的小区间。\n我们给出的不是一个近似值，而是一个能保证根所在位置的区间。|
|为什么不使用网格搜索？\nWhy don't you use grid search?|Grid search can miss a crossing point between two grid points.\nIt also gives only an approximate location of the crossing.\nOur method finds the roots of the polynomial directly, so it can identify all real roots on the specified path.|网格搜索可能会漏掉两个网格点之间的交叉点。\n而且它给出的交叉位置也只是近似值。\n我们的方法直接求多项式的根，因此能够找出指定路径上所有的实根。|
|什么是「完整证书」（complete certificate）？\nWhat does "complete certificate" mean?|It means that for the fixed prediction table and the specified path, we can identify all pairwise crossing points and ranking intervals.\nWe do not need to choose a grid resolution.|这意味着，对于固定的预测表和指定的路径，我们能够找出所有成对的交叉点和排名区间。\n我们不需要选择网格分辨率。|
|什么是「依赖于人工产物」（artifact-conditional）？\nWhat does "artifact-conditional" mean?|It means that the certificate is exact for the fixed prediction artifact used in the evaluation.\nWe do not claim that the result is universally true for all datasets or all future predictions.|这意味着该证书对于评估中使用的固定预测结果是精确成立的。\n我们并不宣称这个结论对所有数据集或未来的所有预测都普遍成立。|
|为什么有理数端点很重要？\nWhy are rational endpoints important?|The group weights at the two endpoints are rational numbers because they are based on counts.\nTherefore, we can keep the calculations as exact fractions instead of floating-point numbers.\nThis allows us to obtain exact polynomial coefficients and rigorous root intervals.|两个端点上的组权重都是有理数，因为它们是基于计数得到的。\n因此，我们可以把计算过程保持为精确的分数，而不是浮点数。\n这使我们能够得到精确的多项式系数和严格的根区间。|
|「三次是紧的」（degree three is tight）是什么意思？\nWhat does "degree three is tight" mean?|It means that three is not only an upper bound.\nWe can construct a valid example where the resulting polynomial actually has degree three.\nTherefore, the bound cannot be reduced to two in general.|这意味着三次不仅仅是一个上界。\n我们可以构造出一个有效的例子，使得最终的多项式确实达到三次。\n因此，这个界限在一般情况下不能降低到二次。|
|第一节的最终结论是什么？\nWhat is the final result of Section 1?|The main conclusion is that, for a fixed prediction table and a specified affine weighting path, the equality between two binary macro-F1 scores can be reduced to a polynomial equation of degree at most three. Therefore, we can find all crossing points exactly and determine whether each crossing is a real ranking reversal or only a tangential tie.|主要结论是：对于固定的预测表和指定的仿射加权路径，两个二分类macro-F1分数相等的条件可以转化为一个次数最多为三次的多项式方程。因此，我们能够精确地找出所有交叉点，并判断每个交叉点究竟是真正的排名反转，还是仅仅是相切平局。|
